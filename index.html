How it fits together

One order, followed from table to report.

Each step hands the right information to the next team — no re-typing, no chasing.

  1. 1

    The order is taken

    At the counter, at the table with the waiter app, or online — every order lands in the same place, with modifiers and notes intact.

    Point of Sale · Waiter App · Online Ordering
  2. 2

    The kitchen gets a clear ticket

    Items are routed to the right station and preparation status is visible to the floor, so nobody has to walk back and ask.

    Kitchen Display
  3. 3

    The guest pays without friction

    Order totals and checkout live in the same flow, including split bills for tables that want them.

    Payments
  4. 4

    Stock and reports catch up

    Sales and ingredient usage feed your reports, so tomorrow’s purchasing and menu decisions start from today’s activity.

    Inventory · Reports & BI
