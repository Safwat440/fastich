Operations

A practical guide to building a smoother restaurant shift

Map the handoffs between front of house, kitchen and management.

1 min read Fastich editorial

Restaurant operations are made up of many small decisions. The most useful improvements usually begin by making the current process visible, agreeing on a clear owner for each step, and checking whether the change makes service easier for the team.

Start with the workflow

Map what happens today, from the moment an order is received to the moment it is completed. Note where information is repeated, where people have to ask for updates, and where delays are most common.

Make the next step clear

Keep responsibilities simple. Give the team a shared view of status and define what should happen when an order, stock item or customer request needs attention.

Review and improve

Choose a small set of indicators that help you understand whether the process is working. Review them regularly with the people closest to the work.
