Fastich Point of Sale

POS & Restaurant Management

Run every shift from one connected workspace. Take orders, manage tables, coordinate teams and understand your daily performance.

Ask the team All products
Made for the way you work

The counter, the floor and the shift — in one place.

Fastich Point of Sale is where most of the day starts: orders are taken, tables are managed, staff work within their roles and the day’s activity is recorded for reporting.

  • Menus with modifiers, combos and notes
  • Tables, tabs and split bills
  • Staff roles and permissions
  • Discounts and promotions applied consistently
