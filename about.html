About Fastich

Technology should make hospitality feel more human

Fastich is a restaurant technology platform built around clarity, useful workflows and room to grow.

Ask the team Explore products
Our point of view

Great tools should get out of the team’s way.

Restaurant teams balance guests, timing, ingredients, people and a thousand small decisions. A connected system should make those decisions easier to see and easier to act on.

What we value
  • Clarity over clutter
  • Workflows built around people
  • Useful information at the right time
  • Growth without unnecessary complexity
Principles

How we build Fastich.

A product direction shaped around the reality of restaurant work.

Clarity

Make the next action obvious, for every person on shift.

Connection

Help teams share context across counter, floor, kitchen and office.

Practicality

Focus on the work people actually do every day.
