Using menu data to make better decisions

Learn how item performance and customer patterns can guide planning.

1 min read Fastich editorial

Your sales history already shows which dishes earn their place on the menu. Looking at it regularly turns menu changes from guesswork into a routine decision.

Look at popularity and margin together

An item that sells often but costs a lot to make may contribute less than a quieter dish with a better margin. Review both numbers side by side before deciding what to promote, reprice or remove.

Watch for patterns over time

Compare the same period across weeks rather than single days. Changes by daypart, weekday or season often explain why an item looks strong one week and weak the next.

Change one thing at a time

When you adjust a price, a description or a dish’s position on the menu, give it enough time to show an effect before changing something else. Keep a short note of what changed and when, so later results are easier to read.
