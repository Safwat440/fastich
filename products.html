Front of house

Take and serve orders

Point of Sale

Fastich Point of Sale is where most of the day starts: orders are taken, tables are managed, staff work within their roles and the day’s activity is recorded for reporting.

Explore Point of Sale

Waiter App

The Waiter App helps floor teams capture orders at the table and coordinate service with fewer handoffs.

Explore Waiter App

Online Ordering

Online Ordering gives guests a simple way to browse your menu, order ahead and choose pickup or delivery — with orders flowing into the same kitchen queue as everything else.

Explore Online Ordering
Back of house

Prepare, stock and pay

Kitchen Display

The Kitchen Display turns incoming orders from the counter, tables and online channels into a single kitchen workflow, from ticket routing to preparation status.

Explore Kitchen Display

Inventory

Inventory tracks ingredients, purchasing and stock movement, giving you a clearer view of costs and of what needs reordering.

Explore Inventory

Payments

Payments brings order totals and checkout workflows together, so closing a table or a counter order is part of the same flow rather than a separate step.

Explore Payments
